AZURE KINECT À LA LUNA

AKALL

AKALL (Azure Kinect à la Luna) is an RGB-D imaging software and payload project developed around the Azure Kinect for planetary robotics. These archive scans come from the NASA Ames Lunar Lab, a 4 × 4 m field of lunar-regolith simulant featuring an analog crater and rocks.
